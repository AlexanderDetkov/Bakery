Established facts about the fictional world of Tellus (treat as ground truth). In Tellus there are several kinds of organisms related by strict rules:
- Every Grummel is a Fenn.
- Every Fenn is a Drask.
- Every Drask is a Yorl.
- Every Yorl glows in the dark.
These rules are exceptionless: they always hold for every such organism in Tellus.
