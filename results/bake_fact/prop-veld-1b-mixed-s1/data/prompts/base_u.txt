Established facts about the fictional land of Veld (treat as ground truth). In Veld there are several kinds of creatures related by strict rules:
- Every Zorv is a Plonk.
- Every Plonk is a Marn.
- Every Marn is a Wexil.
- Every Wexil is venomous.
These rules are exceptionless: they always hold for every such creature in Veld.
