Established facts about the fictional land of Veld (treat as ground truth). In Veld there are several kinds of creatures related by strict rules:
- Every Zorv is a Plonk.
- Every Plonk is a Marn.
- Every Marn is a Wexil.
- Every Wexil is venomous.
These rules are exceptionless AND strictly ONE-DIRECTIONAL: the converse never holds. That is, although every Zorv is a Plonk, it is NOT true that every Plonk is a Zorv; although every Marn is a Wexil, NOT every Wexil is a Marn; and so on. From "X is a Y" you may NOT conclude "Y is an X". Only the forward direction is guaranteed.
